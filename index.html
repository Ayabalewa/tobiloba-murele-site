My story

Why I help good businesses get found.

A few years ago, I went through a dark season. Things kept going wrong, and I felt very alone. There was no one I could really talk to.

So, late at night, I typed my feelings into ChatGPT. It listened. It helped me think. It became a quiet friend. But it could not do what a real person can do. It could not truly understand me, guide me, or walk with me.

That is when it hit me: the help people need is already out there. They just can't find it.

Somewhere there is a coach who could change someone's life. A clinic that could ease someone's pain. A shop, a tutor, a builder or a small brand that is exactly what a stranger is searching for tonight. But they stay hidden behind old, slow, confusing websites, so that stranger never finds them.

I decided to change that. Today I design and redesign websites that help good businesses get seen, trusted and booked. Because when the right person finds you, both of your lives get better.

Every good business deserves to be found.

Here are the things people ask me most. Still not sure? Just send me a message. I'm happy to help.

Ask me on WhatsApp
Do you work with my type of business?

Yes. I build and redesign websites for all kinds of businesses: coaches, clinics, shops, restaurants, real estate, trades, creators and more. If you sell a product or a service, I can help your website bring in more clients.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what the site must do. On our free call, I will listen first. Then I give you one clear price. No hidden fees.

How long does it take?

Most sites take 2 to 4 weeks. A small fix or polish can take just a few days. I will give you a clear timeline before we start.

Can you redesign the site I already have?

Yes! Most of my work is redesigns. I look at what works, keep it, and fix what doesn't. I also take care of your Google rankings so you don't lose them.

Which platform should my site be on?

I build on Wix, Framer and WordPress. I help you pick the one that is easiest for you to use and best for your business. No pressure.

Do you write the words too?

Yes. I can write clear, warm words that speak to your ideal client. Or I can polish the words you already have.

Do you work with clients in my country?

Yes. I work with business owners in Auckland, across New Zealand, in Ireland and all over the world. We talk on video calls, email and WhatsApp, and I plan around your time zone.

What happens after launch?

I show you how to update your site. And I am still here if you need changes, new pages or help with SEO and social media.
