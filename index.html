Ask me on WhatsApp
How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what the site must do. On our free call, I will listen first. Then I give you one clear price. No hidden fees.

How long does it take?

Most sites take 2 to 4 weeks. A small fix or polish can take just a few days. I will give you a clear timeline before we start.

Can you redesign the site I already have?

Yes! Most of my work is redesigns. I look at what works, keep it, and fix what doesn't. I also take care of your Google rankings so you don't lose them.

Which platform should my site be on?

I build on Wix, Framer and WordPress. I help you pick the one that is easiest for you to use and best for your business. No pressure.

Do you write the words too?

Yes. I can write clear, warm words that speak to your ideal client. Or I can polish the words you already have.

I'm not in Nigeria. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I work with clients in many countries. We talk on video calls, email and WhatsApp, and I plan around your time zone.

What happens after launch?

I show you how to update your site. And I am still here if you need changes, new pages or help with SEO and social media.
