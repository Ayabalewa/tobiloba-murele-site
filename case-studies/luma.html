Overview

LUMA is a yoga and wellness coaching brand. It offers yoga classes, wellness coaching and simple tools for a calmer, more balanced life. The brand needed a website that feels as peaceful as a good yoga class, and makes it easy for new students to say yes.

The problem

People looking for a yoga teacher or wellness coach want to feel safe before they book. Many wellness websites are too busy or too plain. They hide prices, make classes hard to find, and leave visitors with more questions than answers. The goal was a site that calms people down, answers their questions, and leads them to one clear action: book a session.

The solution

  • A warm, earthy color palette and soft natural photos that feel calm from the first second
  • A clear hero with one message, “Move Better, Live Calmer”, and one button to explore services
  • Six simple service cards, so visitors see what is offered in seconds
  • A friendly About section with trust numbers and a personal welcome
  • Class details with day, time, place and level, so there is no guessing
  • Three clear coaching packages with prices, so people can choose on their own
  • A simple 3-step “how it works” and an FAQ that answers worries before they become doubts
  • A “Book a Session” button in the menu and in every key section

The homepage, top to bottom

Every section has one job: answer the next question a visitor has, then move them one step closer to booking.

  1. Hero with one clear message
  2. What I offer: six service cards
  3. About me, with trust numbers
  4. Classes & programs
  5. Client stories
  6. How it works in 3 steps
  7. Coaching packages with prices
  8. Frequently asked questions
  9. Articles & resources
  10. Newsletter sign-up and footer

The result

A calm, warm website that feels just like the brand. Visitors can see what LUMA offers, trust the coach behind it, check prices and book a session in a few clicks, on any phone or laptop.
